Welcome! This is a sample post to try out the blog's email updates.

I'll use this space to share notes on software engineering, AI, and the things I build and learn. Expect practical examples, lessons from projects, and ideas worth exploring further.

## From the blog to your inbox

When a new article is published, confirmed subscribers receive an email with its title, a short summary, and a link to read the full post here.

If you arrived from that email, you've made it through the first sample update. Thanks for reading!

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of an update email.
